# Third-party media notices

The PWA includes selected exercise illustrations from [Workout Guide](https://github.com/bryllim/workout-guide), created by Bryl Lim and based on the [Everkinetic exercise artwork](https://github.com/everkinetic/data). The artwork is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The selected PNG frames are redistributed with their attribution and asset license in `public/exercise-media/workout-guide/`. The distributed `manifest.json` records source URLs and attribution for selected frames; `mappings.json` lists the app exercise IDs paired with each guide illustration and distinguishes exact name/equipment matches from reviewed barbell aliases. The PWA does not modify the distributed PNG files; it inverts their white line art in CSS for display on its white media canvas.

The Workout Guide artwork retains its CC BY-SA 4.0 terms. This notice concerns those media assets only; it does not change the license of the application's code.
