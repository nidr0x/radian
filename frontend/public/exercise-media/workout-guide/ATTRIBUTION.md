Workout Guide is created and maintained by [Bryl Lim](https://bryllim.com).

Exercise frames are made and curated for Workout Guide by Bryl Lim and are
available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

The PWA distributes 125 selected Workout Guide illustrations. The 40 added in
this update are copied from [Workout Guide commit
`aac599224bb9780305239607ef98540b7e0ce389`](https://github.com/bryllim/workout-guide/tree/aac599224bb9780305239607ef98540b7e0ce389).
Where a frame is an adaptation of artwork from
[Everkinetic](https://github.com/everkinetic/data), its source URL, license,
and changes are retained in the distributed [`manifest.json`](./manifest.json).

The `mappings.json` file links all 80 distinct exercise IDs used by the 60
routine programs to illustrations. Its `match` field notes close equipment
or stance variations where Workout Guide does not provide the exact variant.
The PNG frames are distributed unchanged; the PWA inverts their white line art
on screen using CSS.
