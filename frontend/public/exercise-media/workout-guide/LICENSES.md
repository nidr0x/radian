This PWA distribution contains selected Workout Guide exercise artwork, its
attribution, and its exercise mapping. It does not include the upstream source
code or documentation.

- The distributed PNG frames are licensed under
  [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); the included
  [`LICENSE-ASSETS`](./LICENSE-ASSETS) carries the asset notice.
- Attribution and source URLs for the selected frames are recorded in
  [`ATTRIBUTION.md`](./ATTRIBUTION.md) and [`manifest.json`](./manifest.json).
- [`mappings.json`](./mappings.json) records the app exercise IDs paired with
  the local guide slugs.
